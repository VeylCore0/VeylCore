# Store robbery (`veyl_robbery`)

| Target | Needs | Time | Take |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Register** (at the clerk) | A weapon in hand | 25 s | $400 – $900 cash |
| **Safe** (back room, Innocence Blvd) | A lockpick (25 % chance it breaks) | 60 s | $1,500 – $3,500 cash |

- At least **2 on-duty police** (`MinPolice`); on-duty officers cannot rob.
- The moment it starts, police get an urgent **Store robbery** dispatch call with the location (silent alarm).
- Cooldowns: 30 min per store and target, 10 min per robber.
- Walking away, going down or being cuffed before it finishes gets nothing.

Stores, rewards, times and cooldowns are in `config.lua`.
