# Fishing (`veyl_fishing`)

Anyone can fish. Buy a **Fishing Rod** ($150) and **Fishing Bait** ($3) at a 24/7.

1. Go to a fishing spot (map blips): **Del Perro Pier**, **Alamo Sea**, **Paleto Cove**. Each has its own fish.
2. Face the water and use the rod from your inventory.
3. Wait for the bite (6–16 s), then press **E** within 1.6 s. Too early or too late and it gets away. A catch uses one bait.
4. Sell everything at the **Fish market** (blip) for cash.

| Fish | Price | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mackerel | $35 | Pier, Paleto |
| Sea Bass | $55 | Pier, Alamo |
| Catfish | $45 | Alamo |
| Trout | $60 | Alamo |
| Salmon | $90 | Paleto |
| Tuna | $140 | Pier, Paleto |
| Small Shark | $400 | Pier, Paleto (rare) |

Some casts bring up junk. You can carry up to 50 fish. Spots, catch tables and prices are in `config.lua`.
