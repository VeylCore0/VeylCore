# VeylCore Roadmap

VeylCore is a custom FiveM roleplay framework built from the ground up. This roadmap follows the development plan's milestones. Each milestone has to work before the city grows.

## ✅ Shipped: foundation
- **Core:** characters, money with a full ledger, jobs and gangs (multi-job), permissions, saving and recovery.
- **Character select**, an appearance editor with clothing stores and barbers, and spawning.
- **HUD** with a working seatbelt.
- **Needs:** hunger and thirst.
- **Synced world:** shared time and weather.
- **Interaction systems:** targeting, menus, notifications and dialogs.
- **Inventory:** our own design, with a ground drop box, stashes, usable items and item art.
- **Staff tools and reports.**
- **Loading screen** with Veyl Radio.

## ✅ Shipped: economy and vehicles
- **Banking:** bank and ATM screens, transfers and history.
- **Shops** with server-side prices.
- **GoPostal delivery job:** routes and payouts that can only pay once.
- **Vehicles:** keys, fuel, and all 876 base-game cars.
- **Garages and impound**, with car pictures.
- **Premium Deluxe Motorsport dealership.**
- **LS Customs mechanics:** repairs that use parts, and invoices.

## ✅ Shipped: life in the city
- **Smartphone:**
  - numbers and contacts;
  - messages with sent, delivered and read receipts;
  - group chats and calls (calls need a voice resource);
  - notifications;
  - App Store.
- **Phone apps:** Flock (social), Bank, Garage, Marketplace, Notes and Calculator.
- **Starlite Motel apartments:**
  - 30 rooms you can lease, each a private home;
  - roommates and guest passes;
  - a doorbell for visitors;
  - storage and a wardrobe;
  - furniture placement;
  - parcels and a noticeboard.
- **Door locks:** set up in game, access by job, gang, key or item, business hours, lockpicking and police rams.
- **Growing:** weed, coca and mushrooms. Quality grades, a Grow Journal, supplier stores, and tables for rolling joints, bagging and processing.

## ✅ Built, now testing: closed city alpha
- **City Hall:** ID and licence cards, licences, the job board and showing your ID.
- **Management and boss menus:** hire, fire, promote and employee lists for jobs and gangs.
- **Medical:** being downed, EMS revives, first aid kits and hospital recovery.
- **Voice:** proximity voice and phone calls through pma-voice.
- **Emotes:** search, favorites, props and shared emotes.
- **Next:** in-game acceptance testing with real players.

## 🔜 Then: emergency services
- **Dispatch:** calls, blips, unit status and a panic button.
- **Police:** duty, cuffs, escort and search, citations and impound.
- **MDT:** people and vehicle lookups, reports and citations.
- **Prison:** sentences that persist, and controlled release.
- **EMS:** injuries, treatment, beds and medical items.

## 🔜 Then: public beta
- **Businesses:** ownership, employees, stock and workstations.
- **A second civilian job:** recycling, fishing, trucking or taxi.
- **The first robbery:** a store robbery with dispatch and cooldowns.
- **Migration tools** for servers coming from QBCore or QBox.
- **Full restart, backup and clean-install checks.**

## 🌱 Later
- **More crime:**
  - drugs: branding, meth, ecstasy, LSD and Veyl-X, dealing and territory;
  - boosting and a black market;
  - gang activities.
- **Phone:** camera and gallery, services, maps, property and job apps.
- **Crafting**, plus inventory weapons, a hotbar and vehicle trunks.
- **More housing** beyond the Starlite Motel.
- **Skills, racing and a lottery.**
