# VeylCore Roadmap

VeylCore is a custom FiveM roleplay framework built from the ground up. This roadmap follows the development plan's milestones. Each milestone has to work before the city grows.

## ✅ Shipped: foundation
- **Core:** characters, money with a full ledger, jobs and gangs (multi-job), permissions, saving and recovery.
- **Character select**, an appearance editor with clothing stores and barbers, and spawning.
- **HUD** with a working seatbelt.
- **Needs:** hunger and thirst.
- **Synced world:** shared time and weather.
- **Interaction systems:** targeting, menus, notifications and dialogs.
- **Inventory:** our own design, with a ground drop box, stashes, usable items and item art.
- **Staff tools and reports.**
- **Loading screen** with Veyl Radio.

## ✅ Shipped: economy and vehicles
- **Banking:** bank and ATM screens, transfers and history.
- **Shops** with server-side prices.
- **GoPostal delivery job:** routes and payouts that can only pay once.
- **Vehicles:** keys, fuel, and all 876 base-game cars.
- **Garages and impound**, with car pictures.
- **Premium Deluxe Motorsport dealership.**
- **LS Customs mechanics:** repairs that use parts, and invoices.

## ✅ Shipped: life in the city
- **Smartphone:** numbers and contacts, messages with read receipts, group chats, calls, notifications and an App Store.
- **Phone apps:** Flock (social), Bank, Garage, Marketplace, Notes and Calculator.
- **Starlite Motel apartments:** 30 rooms you walk into with your key, roommates, guest passes, a doorbell, storage, a wardrobe, furniture, parcels and a noticeboard.
- **Door locks:** set up in game, access by job, gang, key or item, business hours, lockpicking and police rams.
- **Growing:** weed, coca and mushrooms, with quality grades, a Grow Journal, supplier stores and processing tables.

## ✅ Built, now testing: closed city alpha
- **City Hall:** ID and licence cards with your photo, licences, the job board and showing your ID.
- **Boss menus:** hire by offer, fire, promote and employee lists for jobs and gangs.
- **Medical:** being downed, a custom death screen, EMS revives, first aid kits and hospital recovery.
- **Voice:** proximity voice and phone calls through pma-voice.
- **Emotes:** about 90 emotes with search, favorites, props and shared emotes.

## ✅ Built, now testing: emergency services
- **Dispatch:** call cards and blips, unit status and callsigns, a panic button, and 911 / 311 for everyone.
- **Police:** cuffs, escort, search, citations, impound, Check ID and gunshot residue tests.
- **Departments:** rank-locked police and EMS vehicle garages and equipment lockers.
- **MDT:** people and vehicle lookups, warrants, BOLOs, reports, a penal code with fine and sentence totals, a roster and licence control.
- **Prison:** sentences that persist, yard work, a canteen, belongings kept until release, and optional escapes.
- **EMS:** injuries per body part, bleeding, treatment, hospital beds, medical items, pay per revive and medical bills.

## ✅ Built, now testing: public beta
- **Weapons:** 50 weapons and 5 ammo types as inventory items, drawn from the hotbar, with ammo used per shot.
- **Stores:** every 24/7, LTD Gasoline, Rob's Liquor and Ammu-Nation (weapon licence required).
- **Businesses:** Bean Machine and Burger Shot with owners, staff, a business account, stock, workstations and a register.
- **Fishing:** three fishing spots with their own fish, and a fish market.
- **Store robberies:** registers and safes, a police minimum, a silent alarm and cooldowns.
- **Migration tool** from QBCore or QBox, a **server health check** and **database backups**.

## 🛠️ Next up: launch polish
- **In-game acceptance testing** with real players, system by system.
- **Item art** for weapons, ammo, fish, ingredients and medical items.
- **Location pass:** hospital beds, businesses, prison and department points checked against our MLOs.
- **Performance pass:** client and server timings on a full server.
- **Clean install** from the txAdmin recipe, start to finish.

## 🔜 Then: crime expansion
- **Bigger robberies:** Fleeca banks, the jewelry store and the Paleto bank.
- **Drug dealing:** corner selling, buyers who call the police, and turf.
- **More drugs:** branding, meth, ecstasy, LSD and Veyl-X.
- **Boosting** and a **black market** with a dark web phone app.
- **Gangs:** turf, graffiti, gang stashes and money laundering.
- **Weapons:** serial numbers, attachments and illegal weapon dealers.

## 🔜 Then: more jobs and city life
- **New jobs:** trucking, taxi, tow truck, garbage, mining, hunting and farming.
- **Vehicles:** trunks and gloveboxes, plates you can change, ownership transfers and insurance.
- **Crafting:** benches, recipes and materials from the new jobs.
- **More businesses:** a car wash, a nightclub, a pawn shop and a tuner shop.

## 🔜 Then: phone 2.0
- **Camera and gallery:** photos and selfies, albums, and sharing photos in messages and on Flock.
- **Maps:** set GPS to contacts, businesses and saved places, and share your live location.
- **Services:** call or message the police, EMS, mechanics, taxis and businesses, with a queue for whoever is on duty.
- **Email:** receipts, bills, job offers and messages from businesses and the city.
- **Jobs app:** job listings, applications and shift clock-in for the job you work.
- **Property app:** listings, your keys, rent due and roommates.
- **Garage app 2.0:** vehicle tracking, insurance and valet delivery.
- **Bank app 2.0:** invoices and bills to pay, recurring payments and a credit score.
- **Music:** Veyl Radio, playlists and a speaker you can play for people nearby.
- **News app:** Weazel News articles and breaking alerts.
- **Dark web:** a hidden app for black market orders, unlocked by a USB drive.
- **Racing app:** street races, invites and leaderboards.
- **Voicemail, call history and blocked numbers.**
- **Phone looks:** wallpapers, ringtones, cases and dark mode.
- **Tablet:** a bigger screen for the MDT, business management and real estate.

## 🔜 Then: housing and justice
- **Housing:** houses and apartments to buy or rent across the city, keys, storage and furniture.
- **Real estate agents:** listings, viewings and sales.
- **Justice:** lawyers, judges, court cases and fines that go to the city.
- **News:** a Weazel News job with cameras and articles.

## 🌱 Later
- **Racing:** street races with a phone app, tracks and leaderboards.
- **Skills:** stamina, driving, shooting and fishing that improve as you play.
- **Casino**, a **lottery** and arcade games.
- **Web admin panel:** players, characters, logs and bans in a browser.
- **More languages** for every screen.
