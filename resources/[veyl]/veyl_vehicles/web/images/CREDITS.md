# Vehicle images

One picture per base-game model (`<model>.webp`), from the FiveM documentation's vehicle-models reference
(https://docs.fivem.net/docs/game-references/vehicle-references/vehicle-models/). They are renders of Grand Theft Auto V
vehicles (Rockstar Games) and are resized to 360 px wide here to keep the client download small (about 9 MB).

Add-on vehicles: drop a `<model>.webp` (transparent background, about 360x160) in this folder. A missing picture falls back
to a plain icon in the UI.
